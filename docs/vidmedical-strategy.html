Operating principle

CON

Convenience for now or you are stupid

Solo founder. Zero budget. Too many plates. Default to the cheapest, lowest-friction option that gets VidMedical moving. When there is a team to delegate to and revenue to play with, upgrade to options that are better for VM long-term.

Brand

VidMedical is a studio brand — not a personal-name freelance face. Personal name converts faster early (Upwork lane). Brand compounds better: privacy, hireable, sellable equity.

Anonymous / no team headshots is normal agency positioning. Trust comes from work, process, and reliable ops — not from a homepage face. The real risk is a brand site with no proof; fix that with portfolio depth.

Client & offer

Healthcare professionals and practices who need short-form clinical content — substance shorts, not clinic-vibes fluff. Async-friendly so clinic days stay clinic days.

Essential — $67

Clinician-on-camera polish: clean pacing, dynamic subtitles, audio tuning, curated clinical stock B-roll.

Advanced — $199

Advanced edit with 2D/3D elements from prebuilt assets — motion graphics, anatomical inserts, MoA-style overlays. Not custom Cinema 4D builds from scratch.

Your role

Chief medical editor / director Briefs, standards, clinical QA, clients, and business — not keyframing every Essential timeline.

Working with a team is its own skillset: hiring, rates, briefs, asset handoff, QA feedback, client side. Since human help is needed after the first few orders anyway, build team-shaped workflows from day one — not solo-hero first, then flip under fire.

CON applied — AI / MCP

Now

  • No time on building/testing MCP pipelines
  • No heavy in-house template farms or binder SOPs
  • Editors use their own templates/AI if current; they ship authentic videos
  • One editor ↔ one client owns that client’s brand / voice / tone

Later

  • VM-specific tech workflows once there is footing
  • In-house templates and asset libraries
  • Shared SOPs all editors follow

Reality check: for shorts, AI timeline help rarely halves total job time. Taste/QA still costs; pipeline upkeep eats savings. MCP is optional assist — not the capacity strategy.

Thin non-negotiables only: what “done” means (Essential vs Advanced), clinical accuracy bar, and how files reach you for review.

CON applied — hiring

Now — Upwork

Costlier per video, fastest path: hire good + affordable talent worldwide for one video at a time. Escrow, milestones, no salary/HR overhead.

Later — Local Pakistan

Ideal long-term: strong affordable talent (including Rawalpindi). Job posts, interviews, contracts, retainers — when volume and bandwidth exist. That’s VM’s cost edge.

Ops snapshot

  1. Client lands → brief against Essential / Advanced.
  2. Assign one Upwork editor; they own craft + client voice for that account.
  3. You: medical QA + taste bar + client communication.
  4. Delivery async (Drive / unlisted link fine now; Frame.io later if revision chaos warrants it).
